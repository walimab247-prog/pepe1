About Us

Independent Crypto Intelligence

CoinLumen News is a reader-first crypto media outlet delivering timely market data, movements and expert analysis on the assets you follow.

Our Mission

Who We Are

CoinLumen was founded on a simple idea: crypto readers deserve fast, honest and genuinely useful market coverage — free of hype and noise. We combine on-chain data, disciplined technical analysis and clear writing to help both newcomers and seasoned traders make sense of a fast-moving market.

Our editorial standards emphasise accuracy, context and transparency. Every market piece is informational, never financial advice, and we disclose the limits of any analysis so readers can think for themselves. From breaking headlines to deep technical breakdowns, our mission is to light the way through the crypto landscape — the meaning behind our name.

Values

Our Values

Independence, clarity and accountability guide everything we publish. We protect editorial independence from the assets and platforms we cover, we write in plain language, and we correct mistakes openly. If we get something wrong, we fix it and say so.
