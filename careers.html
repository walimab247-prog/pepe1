Culture

Why Work With Us

CoinLumen is a remote-first newsroom that pairs sharp editorial standards with modern tooling. We move fast, ship often and give writers and engineers real ownership over their work. If you love crypto markets and care about getting the details right, you will fit in here.

We offer flexible hours, a learning budget, and a culture that prizes honest feedback over hierarchy. Above all, we want people who can turn complex market events into clear, trustworthy stories our readers rely on.
