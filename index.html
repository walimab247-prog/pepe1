Crypto Market FAQs

Answers to common questions about market trends and news.

What does a wedge pattern mean?

A wedge is a chart pattern formed by two converging trend lines. A falling wedge often signals weakening selling pressure and a possible bullish reversal, while a rising wedge can hint at exhaustion. Traders confirm the signal with a breakout on rising volume.

How do I interpret ETF inflows?

ETF inflows show net new money entering a fund. Sustained positive inflows suggest growing institutional demand and can support price, while outflows indicate the opposite. One strong week matters less than a multi-week trend.

What should I watch for in May?

Watch whether Bitcoin holds its reclaimed moving averages, whether altcoin strength broadens, and whether breakouts arrive on convincing volume. Key support and resistance levels and macro rate expectations will shape sentiment.
