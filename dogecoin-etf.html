Dogecoin ETF Records First Positive Inflows

Dogecoin-linked products log their first positive inflows since June, hinting at renewed institutional curiosity in the original memecoin.

Overview

Inflows Return

Dogecoin exchange-traded products recorded their first positive inflows since June, a modest but notable shift after months of muted demand. While the sums are small relative to Bitcoin and Ethereum vehicles, the change in direction matters for a sentiment-driven asset like DOGE.

Memecoins tend to move on narrative and liquidity, so even early institutional participation can amplify momentum. Analysts caution, however, that a single week of inflows is not a trend, and that follow-through over several weeks is needed to confirm a durable shift.
