XRP and SOL Join Zero-Interest Loans

Nexo expands its zero-interest loan support to include XRP and SOL, broadening borrowing utility for holders of both assets.

Overview

Expanding the Collateral Menu

Nexo has officially expanded its zero-interest loan program to include XRP and SOL, letting holders unlock liquidity against their positions without triggering a taxable sale. The addition widens a collateral menu that already spans major assets and stablecoins.

For long-term holders, borrowing against collateral can be a way to access cash while retaining upside exposure. Zero-interest tiers typically apply within a conservative loan-to-value band, rewarding lower leverage with better terms and reducing the risk of liquidation during volatility.

Highlights

Why It Matters

Three practical benefits for XRP and SOL holders.

Instant Loan Access

Draw liquidity against digital assets without selling.

Secure Collateral

Institution-grade custody and safety protocols for staked tokens.

Multi-Asset Support

XRP and SOL now sit alongside existing supported collateral.

Risk

Borrow Responsibly

Loans are powerful but not risk-free: a sharp drawdown in collateral value can push loan-to-value toward liquidation thresholds. Maintaining a healthy buffer and monitoring positions is essential.
