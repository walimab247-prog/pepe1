Pepe Breaks Key Wedge as Price Nears $0.00000482

PEPE pushes out of a multi-week falling wedge on rising volume, reviving hopes of a short-term trend reversal for the leading memecoin.

Overview

A Falling Wedge Gives Way

Pepe (PEPE) has broken out of a well-defined falling wedge pattern, a structure that traders often read as a sign of fading selling pressure and a possible handover of control back to buyers. The breakout arrived alongside a clear increase in trading volume, suggesting that renewed interest — rather than a thin, low-liquidity drift — is behind the move.

Price is now testing the immediate resistance band around $0.00000482, with the next hurdle sitting just above at $0.00000509. Momentum studies support the cautiously optimistic read: the Relative Strength Index hovers near 57, showing strengthening momentum that is not yet stretched into overbought territory. Short- and medium-term moving averages have begun to tilt bullish, even as the longer-term averages keep the broader structure defensive.

Outlook

Where Price Could Go Next

If PEPE can break and hold above the $0.00000482–$0.00000490 region, analysts see room for a move toward $0.00000520–$0.00000550, in line with the 10–20% post-wedge rallies frequently seen in high-volume memecoins. A sustained close above $0.00000444 would open the door to $0.00000500, $0.00000625 and the channel limit near $0.00000723.

The macro picture, however, still prints lower highs and lower lows, so a genuine reversal needs repeated closes above these levels. Thin volume during a surge can produce a bull trap, and stalling at resistance could invite a corrective pullback toward support. Traders are watching volume and a clean daily close for confirmation.
